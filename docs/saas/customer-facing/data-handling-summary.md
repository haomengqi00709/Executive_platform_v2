# How we handle your data — the short version

**For:** prospective and current SaaS customers who want a one-page
summary before reading the full [privacy policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## What we read

With your Microsoft 365 OAuth consent, we access:

- Your **Outlook email** (inbox and sent).
- Your **calendar** (events, attendees, meeting metadata).
- **OneDrive files** you've authorized (mostly meeting recordings).
- Your 1:1 **Teams chat** with the Audrey assistant bot we provision for
  you.

We never see your Microsoft password — Microsoft handles login for us.

## What we do with it

Five AI assistant modules:

1. **Daily briefing** — calendar + email summary delivered every morning.
2. **Email triage** — sorts, prioritizes, drafts replies to your inbox.
3. **Meeting summary** — transcribes recordings and extracts action items.
4. **Relationship intelligence** — surfaces who you've been talking to and
   who's gone quiet.
5. **Expenses** — finds receipts and invoices in your inbox and files.

## Where your data lives

- **SaaS tier (you're here)**: Railway, US-east region.
- **BYOC tier (alternative)**: your own Azure tenant, your choice of region.

## Who else touches it

Three sub-processors (full list in [subprocessor-list.md](./subprocessor-list.md)):

- **Microsoft** — you're already using them for M365.
- **Google Gemini** — AI processing. Per Gemini's paid terms, your content
  is not retained for model training.
- **Railway** — our hosting provider.

## What you control

- **Delete your account** anytime in Settings → "Delete my account". All
  your data is gone within 7 days (within 30 days from backups).
- **Revoke our access** anytime at
  https://myaccount.microsoft.com → Permissions.
- **Turn off individual modules** in Settings.
