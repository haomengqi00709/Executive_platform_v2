Rank your MOST ACTIVE contacts by two-way email activity (messages received from them + messages you
sent to them) over the past N days. Automated / no-reply / system senders and your own address are
excluded, so this is real people you correspond with — but it is still VOLUME, not curated importance.
For "my key / high-priority / client / internal contacts", use list_crm_contacts (the curated CRM).
Each row shows total activity plus the in/out split and whether the contact is in your CRM.
